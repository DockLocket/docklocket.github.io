DockLocket

Display your favorite photos directly in your Mac Dock. A subtle macOS utility that turns your Dock into a living photo frame, quietly rotating cherished images. Runs locally, stays unobtrusive, and lets you select and customize with ease.

Effortless Photo Rotation

Curate a collection of personal photos—family, friends, memories—and watch them cycle seamlessly in your Dock. No desk required; your Dock becomes the frame.

Full Customization

Adjust frame styles, rotation intervals, and display options to match your aesthetic. Simple controls ensure it integrates perfectly with your workflow.

Local and Lightweight

Everything processes on-device with zero cloud dependency. Minimal resource use means it runs silently in the background, always ready.

Refined Experience

Version 1.0.1 brings polished improvements, updated metadata, and seamless presentation for the smoothest Dock integration yet.

How It Works

Step 1: Select Your Photos

Choose images from your library—personal favorites, travels, or daily joys. Drag and drop or browse directly.

Step 2: Customize the Frame

Tailor the look: pick frame styles, set rotation speed, and fine-tune display settings to your preference.

Step 3: Activate in Dock

Launch DockLocket—it embeds quietly into your Dock, starting the rotation immediately.

Step 4: Enjoy Effortlessly

Photos cycle automatically. Access settings anytime via a simple menu. Runs locally, always unobtrusive.

FAQ

Is DockLocket free to use?

This is a fully activated version—no purchases or subscriptions needed. Ready to go out of the box.

Does it require an internet connection?

No. All processing happens locally on your Mac for complete privacy and reliability.

Can I pause or change photos anytime?

Yes. Right-click the Dock icon for instant access to settings, photo swaps, or pause.

What macOS versions are supported?

Optimized for macOS Sonoma and later, with native Apple Silicon support.

How do I uninstall if needed?

Drag DockLocket to Trash. No remnants or lingering processes.
